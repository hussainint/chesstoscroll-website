How ChessToScroll works

Lock the apps that pull you in. Solve a short checkmate puzzle when your minutes run out. Earn the time back.

Set it up

  1. 1

    Pick the apps that pull you in, such as Instagram, YouTube, or TikTok. Nothing is locked during this first pick.

  2. 2

    Tell the app your chess rating, or how much chess you know if you have no rating. Puzzles are matched to that level.

  3. 3

    Choose a goal: reduce screen time, improve chess, build a daily habit, stay focused, or scroll less at night.

  4. 4

    Grant two Android permissions. Usage access lets the app notice when a locked app opens. Display over other apps lets the puzzle cover that app until it is solved. Both are required for the lock.

  5. 5

    Lock one app now. More can be locked later.

Two permissions, in plain language

The lock needs both. You can turn them off later in Android settings. If either one is off, the lock cannot run.

Usage access

Android uses this so an app can tell which other app is open. ChessToScroll uses it to notice when a locked app opens. It does not read messages, photos, passwords, or what you type.

Display over other apps

This lets the puzzle sit on top of the locked app until you solve it or go home. Without it, the lock cannot cover the screen.

There is no account. Progress, locked apps, the streak, and earned time stay on the phone.

The time bank

Minutes you earn sit in one bank. While the bank has time, locked apps open as usual. When it hits zero, the next open shows a full-screen lock: “Solve a puzzle to unlock.”

  1. 1

    Solve a puzzle.

  2. 2

    Minutes land in the bank.

  3. 3

    Locked apps open while time remains.

  4. 4

    At zero, the puzzle covers the next open.

If one app is locked, that app is unlocked for the remaining time. If several are locked, all of them are unlocked until the bank runs out. Spend the minutes, or keep them for later.

The Time tab also has a button to play a puzzle and earn more time before you open a locked app. Solving adds the minutes and, on the lock screen, opens the app.

The daily streak

The streak continues when you solve at least one puzzle a day. Stats shows the current streak and your best streak, along with puzzles solved and time earned, by day and by week.

Four puzzle levels

Checkmate puzzles. You play one side. The app plays the opponent’s replies. A hint can be shown on the board. Puzzle level can be changed later. Your rating, or how much chess you know, is what matches the puzzles to you.

Inside the app

Three tabs. The same minutes, streak, and locks are on each of them.

Time

Minutes available, the streak, locked apps, and a button to play a puzzle and earn more time. One locked app stays open for the remaining time. Several locked apps all stay open until the bank runs out.

Stats

Daily and weekly views of puzzles solved, time earned, current streak, best streak, and which locked apps used the most time.

Apps

Search the apps installed on your phone. Lock or unlock them.

A self-control tool

ChessToScroll is for the person holding the phone. Beginners and rated players both fit, because difficulty follows the level they give. Parental controls and chess courses are different products.

Questions

How does ChessToScroll lock an app?

You pick the app, then turn the lock on. Nothing is locked during the first pick. When you open a locked app and your minutes are gone, a puzzle covers the screen. You can solve it or go home. Solving adds minutes and opens the app.

Which Android permissions does it need?

Two. Usage access lets ChessToScroll notice when a locked app opens. Display over other apps lets the puzzle cover that app until you solve it or go home. Both are required for the lock.

How many minutes does a puzzle add?

Easy, mate in 1, adds 1 minute by default. Medium, mate in 2, adds 1 minute. Hard, mate in 3, adds 5 minutes. Super Hard, mate in 4, adds 10 minutes. In Profile, Puzzle time can set each level from 1 to 60 minutes.

Does ChessToScroll need an account?

No. Progress, locked apps, the streak, and earned time stay on the phone.

Is ChessToScroll a parental control app?

No. It is a self-control tool for the person using the phone. It is not a chess course.

Is ChessToScroll on iPhone?

Not yet. ChessToScroll is on Android now. There is no iOS link yet.
