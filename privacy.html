Privacy Policy

Last updated June 2026

Welcome to ChessToScroll (“we,” “our,” or “us”). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how ChessToScroll collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

By using ChessToScroll, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

About ChessToScroll

ChessToScroll is a digital wellbeing and screen time management application that helps users reduce distractions by requiring chess challenges before accessing selected apps.

ChessToScroll does not require account creation, registration, or login to use the app.

Information we collect

Information you provide

ChessToScroll does not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Date of birth
  • Profile information

Since there is no login or account system, we do not maintain user accounts.

App usage information

To provide core functionality, the app may process certain information locally on your device, including:

  • Apps selected for blocking or restriction
  • Chess challenge settings
  • Usage preferences
  • Focus and screen-time settings
  • App configuration data

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Device information

Depending on the features used, the app may access limited device-related information such as:

  • Android version
  • Device model
  • App version

This information is used solely to ensure app functionality and compatibility.

Accessibility Service usage

ChessToScroll may use Android Accessibility Services to provide app-blocking and screen control features.

Accessibility permissions are used exclusively to:

  • Detect when a selected app is opened
  • Display chess challenges before access is granted
  • Enforce user-configured screen-time restrictions

ChessToScroll does not use Accessibility Services to:

  • Read personal messages
  • Collect passwords
  • Monitor keystrokes
  • Record screen content
  • Collect personal data for advertising purposes

Accessibility data is processed only as required for the app’s functionality.

Permissions used by ChessToScroll

Accessibility Service permission

ChessToScroll uses Android’s Accessibility Service solely to provide its core app-blocking functionality.

The Accessibility Service is used to:

  • Detect when a blocked application is opened
  • Display a chess challenge before access is granted
  • Enforce user-configured focus sessions and digital detox rules
  • Help users reduce distractions and limit access to selected applications

ChessToScroll does not use Accessibility Services to:

  • Read personal messages
  • Capture text entered in other apps
  • Record passwords
  • Monitor browsing activity
  • Collect sensitive personal information
  • Sell or share user data

All Accessibility Service usage is limited to the functionality described above.

Usage Access Permission

ChessToScroll uses Android’s Usage Access Permission to:

  • Identify which applications are currently active
  • Determine when a blocked application is launched
  • Measure app usage duration for digital wellbeing features
  • Provide screen time insights and statistics

This information is processed locally on the device and is used only to provide the app’s functionality.

Data collection and storage

ChessToScroll does not require user accounts and does not collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Home addresses
  • Payment information

Most data, including app settings, blocked app selections, and screen time preferences, is stored locally on your device.

Data sharing

ChessToScroll does not sell, rent, or share user data with advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

Information collected through Accessibility Services and Usage Access Permission is never sold or used for advertising purposes.

Data security

Any information processed by ChessToScroll is used only for app functionality and is handled using reasonable security practices designed to protect user data.

How we use information

Information processed by ChessToScroll is used solely to:

  • Provide digital detox features
  • Enable app blocking functionality
  • Display chess challenges
  • Store user preferences
  • Improve app performance and reliability

We do not use your information for advertising profiling.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties.

Since ChessToScroll does not maintain user accounts or collect personal information, we generally have no personal data to share.

Third-party services

ChessToScroll may use third-party services for analytics, crash reporting, or app performance monitoring.

These providers may collect limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Examples may include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics

If such services are used, data collection is governed by the respective provider’s privacy policies.

Data storage

Most app data is stored locally on your device.

We do not operate user databases containing personal information.

You may remove locally stored app data at any time by:

  • Clearing app data through Android settings
  • Uninstalling the application

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect app data and maintain the security of our application.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Accessing information about data processing
  • Requesting deletion of data
  • Objecting to certain processing activities

Since ChessToScroll stores most information locally on your device and does not maintain user accounts, many requests can be fulfilled by deleting app data or uninstalling the app.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be reflected by updating the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page.

Continued use of ChessToScroll after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
