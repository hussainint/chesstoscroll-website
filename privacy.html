Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

ChessToScroll is operated by Ray Viral Lab (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the ChessToScroll Android app and this website do with information.

Contact: chesstoscroll@gmail.com

ChessToScroll lets you lock apps you choose and earn time on those apps by solving chess puzzles. There is no account and no login.

Information that stays on your phone

The app stores the following in local storage on your device:

  • Language, chess rating or chess level, and the goals you choose during setup
  • The apps you mark as interesting and the apps you lock
  • Reward minutes, earned time, streaks, puzzles solved, and a local log of earned time and time used in locked apps

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this from the phone. We do not sell it.

Apps on your device

If you grant the permissions, the app can:

  • See the list of installed apps, so you can pick which ones to lock
  • Check which app is in the foreground, so it can show the lock screen for apps you locked
  • Read how long apps have been in the foreground over the last 7 days, so the Apps tab can show a Most used group with those times
  • Draw over other apps, so the lock screen can cover a locked app
  • Run a foreground service and start again after the phone restarts, so the lock can keep working

The app does not use Android’s Accessibility Service. It does not read messages, passwords, or what you type in other apps.

Analytics

ChessToScroll uses PostHog, hosted in the United States at https://us.i.posthog.com, to understand how the app is used and to fix problems. PostHog may receive:

  • An anonymous identifier and app opens
  • Language, chess rating or level, and the goals you selected
  • Package names of apps you show interest in, lock, unlock, or that open the lock screen
  • Puzzle events, such as difficulty, whether you solved or left a puzzle, and whether you used a hint
  • Streak, puzzles solved, and earned minutes
  • Whether you granted Usage access or Display over other apps
  • For the Most used group, the package name, minutes of use, and rank of those apps

We use this to operate and improve ChessToScroll. We do not sell it, and we do not use it to build an advertising profile.

Other services

The app may request the Poppins font from Google Fonts. Google can receive your IP address as part of that request.

If you rate the app from the in-app prompt, or open the Play Store listing, Google handles that under its own terms.

If you share the app, the content goes only to the app you pick.

This website

If you open this site, the host may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, and the page requested, so the site can run and stay secure.

Children

ChessToScroll is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email chesstoscroll@gmail.com and we will delete it.

How long we keep information

Local app data stays on the device until you clear it or uninstall the app. Analytics events stay in PostHog for as long as we need them to understand and fix the product. You can ask us to delete analytics linked to you by emailing chesstoscroll@gmail.com. Because there is no account, include the approximate time and the apps involved so we can find the record.

Your choices

  • You can turn off Usage access and Display over other apps in Android settings. The lock will not work without them.
  • You can clear the app’s storage or uninstall it to delete local data.
  • You can email chesstoscroll@gmail.com to ask what analytics we hold or to ask us to delete it.

If privacy law where you live gives you the right to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing, or to complain to a regulator, you can use those rights. For people in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we rely on our legitimate interest in understanding and securing the app for analytics, and on providing the lock you set up for processing on the device.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the date above. If a change materially expands what we collect, we will also surface it in the app when that is practical.
