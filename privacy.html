Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

This policy covers the ChessToScroll Android app and the website at chesstoscroll.com. The iOS app does not have a public link yet. This policy will cover it when it does.

Who we are

ChessToScroll is published by Ray Viral Lab (A Sarwer Hussain), 23/12, Ramasamy Street, Periamet, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600003, India.

Email: support@chesstoscroll.com

The short version

The app has no account. Your locked apps, streak, puzzle progress, and earned minutes stay on your phone. We do not sell personal information. The website does not run advertising or analytics cookies.

What stays on your phone

ChessToScroll stores the following on the device, so the app can lock apps and show your stats:

  • Apps you lock or unlock
  • The chess level you set, and the goal you choose
  • Minutes in the time bank, and the reward you set for each puzzle level
  • Puzzles solved, time earned, current streak, and best streak

We do not receive a copy. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes it from the phone.

Permissions

Usage access

Used on the device to notice when a locked app opens. It is not used to read messages, photos, passwords, or what you type inside other apps.

Display over other apps

Used to show the puzzle over a locked app until you solve it or go home.

Both are required for the lock. ChessToScroll does not ask you to create an account, and it does not ask for your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not share app activity with third parties for their advertising.
  • We do not build an account profile. There is no account.

On ChessToScroll’s Google Play listing, the Data safety section is declared as no data collected and no data shared. That declaration matches this policy.

This website

chesstoscroll.com is a static site. It does not use analytics, advertising cookies, or a sign-in. The typeface is stored with the site, so loading a page does not call a font service.

The company that hosts the site may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP address, date, and the page requested, so the site can stay online and secure. Those logs are not used to build a marketing profile.

If you email support@chesstoscroll.com, we use your message and email address to reply and to handle the request. We do not add you to a mailing list.

Children

ChessToScroll is a tool for the person who uses the phone. It is rated Everyone on Google Play. It is not a parental control service, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can unlock an app, change the puzzle level and rewards, or uninstall ChessToScroll at any time. Android settings can revoke Usage access and Display over other apps. If those permissions are off, the lock cannot run.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. The current version stays on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@chesstoscroll.com
