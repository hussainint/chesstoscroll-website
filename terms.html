The service

ChessToScroll locks apps you choose on your phone. When your earned minutes are gone, you solve a chess puzzle to earn minutes back. You can spend those minutes on the locked apps or keep them for later.

The Android app on Google Play is com.hintapp.chesslock. An iOS version does not have a public link yet. When it does, these terms cover it as well.

Minutes

Minutes are access time inside the app. They are not money, they have no cash value, and they cannot be transferred or redeemed. Default rewards are 1 minute for Easy, 1 minute for Medium, 5 minutes for Hard, and 10 minutes for Super Hard. You can set each level from 1 to 60 minutes in Profile, under Puzzle time.

Chess puzzles

Puzzles are checkmate exercises matched to the level you give the app. You play one side. The app plays the opponent’s replies. A hint may be shown on the board. Practice can support your chess. The app does not promise a rating gain, a study result, or a health result.
