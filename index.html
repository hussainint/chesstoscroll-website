Lock apps. Solve a puzzle. Earn minutes.

  1. 1 Lock apps

    Pick the apps that pull you in, such as Instagram, YouTube, or TikTok.

  2. 2 Solve a puzzle

    Open a locked app with no minutes left and the screen says “Solve a puzzle to unlock.” Solve it, or go home.

  3. 3 Earn minutes

    Solving adds minutes and opens the app. Spend them on the locked apps, or keep them for later. One puzzle a day keeps the streak.

Lock one app today.

Nothing is locked during the first pick. You choose when a lock turns on. More apps can be locked later.

How rewards, hints, and level matching work

Who it is for

People who want to scroll less, and who will solve a short chess puzzle to earn the next few minutes.

Scroll less

The minutes you earn are the minutes you can spend. When the bank is empty, the puzzle asks you to pause.

Your level

Beginners and rated players both fit. You enter a rating, or how much chess you know, and the puzzles follow that.

On your phone

There is no account. Locked apps, the streak, and earned time stay on the device.

ChessToScroll is a self-control tool for your own phone. Parental controls and chess courses are different products.
